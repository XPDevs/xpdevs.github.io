Our Custom Kernel

The driving force behind our next-generation operating systems.

Welcome to XPDevs: Announcing NexShell

At XPDevs, we are thrilled to introduce NexShell, our state-of-the-art open source kernel, the driving force behind our next-generation operating systems. Designed with advanced technology and meticulous attention to detail, this kernel is set to redefine performance, security, and versatility in computing.

What is NexShell?

NexShell is a sophisticated piece of technology developed to be the foundation of our upcoming operating systems. It integrates cutting-edge features to deliver exceptional performance and security, tailored to meet the needs of modern computing environments.

Key Features:

  • Outstanding Performance: Our kernel is optimized for high efficiency, delivering superior performance across a wide range of applications and workloads.
  • Advanced Security: With built-in robust security features, our kernel is designed to protect your systems against emerging threats and vulnerabilities.
  • Scalability: Whether for personal devices or large-scale enterprise environments, our kernel scales efficiently to handle diverse computing needs.
  • Flexibility: Its modular design allows for customization and adaptation, providing a versatile solution for various use cases.

Open Source and Community Driven

We have made the exciting decision to make NexShell fully open source. NexShell is proudly based on basekernel by dthain, extended and optimized for the XPDevs ecosystem.

Get Involved:

  • Download: You can download our operating systems powered by NexShell from our Download Page.
  • NexShell Source Code: Explore and contribute to NexShell on GitHub.
  • Basekernel: Check out the original basekernel project by dthain on GitHub.

Our Commitment to Excellence

At XPDevs, we are dedicated to delivering exceptional technology solutions. Our proprietary kernel represents our commitment to providing a secure, high-performance foundation for the next generation of operating systems.
