About XPDevs

I am the developer behind XPDevs, a project dedicated to building operating systems that are fast, simple, and respect the user. Everything is built from the kernel up, avoiding unnecessary complexity to ensure the code is efficient and secure.

My goal is to make technology open and understandable. I believe that no one should ever have to pay for software, which is why every XPDevs project is, and always will be, free and open source.

Thank you for checking out my work and being part of this vision for what technology should be: simple, open, and built with care.
