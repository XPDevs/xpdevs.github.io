About XPDevs

I'm the developer behind XPDevs, a project where I build operating systems that are fast, simple, and actually respect the user. I start from the kernel and work up, keeping things as straightforward as possible with no unnecessary complexity, just clean, secure code. My aim is to keep technology open and easy to understand. I don't think anyone should have to pay for software, so everything from XPDevs is and will always be free and open source. Thanks for checking out my work and for being part of a different way of thinking about technology. I believe it should be simple, open, and made with care.
