Announcement from XPDevs

At XPDevs, we are excited to announce a major overhaul of DoorsOS.

We have decided to completely refound the Doors Operating System. This means that the old batch file–based versions will no longer be developed, and future releases will focus solely on bootable ISO versions.

Additionally, DoorsOS® (powered by NexShell) will adopt a new versioning system starting from version 1. Technically, this new version 1 will be the fourth iteration of DoorsOS® (powered by NexShell), marking a fresh start for the OS.

We appreciate your continued support as we work towards making DoorsOS more powerful and accessible than ever.

About XPDevs

I'm the developer behind XPDevs — a project dedicated to creating innovative, accessible, and powerful operating systems. My goal is to design software that runs efficiently, simplifies tasks, and enhances the user experience across a wide range of devices.

Everything at XPDevs is built by me, from the kernel up. I use the latest tools and technologies to ensure that each release is stable, secure, and tailored for real-world use. My work is driven by a passion for computing and a desire to make technology more approachable for everyone.

I also provide resources and documentation to help users get the most out of my operating systems. Whether you're exploring DoorsOS for the first time or deploying it in a serious environment, I'm here to support your journey.

XPDevs is more than just software — it's a vision for a better, simpler, and more open tech future, crafted by one person with a commitment to excellence.

Thank you for being part of this journey. Let’s build something amazing together.
