Why work at XPDevs?

We are building technology that respects the person using it. Every role has a clear purpose and a visible result.

Work on real products

DoorsOS, ExamOS, Genesis AI, and NexShell are made to be used. You can see the impact of your work and hear feedback from real people.

Clear ownership

You will be trusted with meaningful work, direct access to project decisions, and honest feedback that helps you grow.

Simple and open

We value clear code, plain language, responsible security, and software that remains useful on hardware people already own.

Life at a small software company

XPDevs stays small by choice. That means less management, more practical responsibility, and direct access to the person making product and company decisions.

Broad ownership

You will often work across different areas. The IT role, for example, covers equipment, networks, accounts, support, security, and practical office systems.

Direct communication

There is less distance between colleagues and decisions. Questions are welcome, feedback is direct, and important information is shared early.

Practical judgement

We choose solutions based on reliability, usefulness, security, cost, and the needs of a small team rather than unnecessary complexity.

Honest support

You will be trusted to work independently, while also expected to ask for help, document decisions, and raise risks early.

Candidate commitments

A professional process should be clear, accessible, and respectful from the first application to the final decision.

Equal opportunity

XPDevs aims to recruit on ability, relevant experience, and potential. We welcome different backgrounds, identities, perspectives, and ways of working.

Accessible process

If you need an adjustment to take part in an interview or discussion, tell us what would help. We will make a reasonable adjustment where possible.

Clear terms

Salary, employment type, location, and working expectations are stated in each role and confirmed in writing before you make a final decision.

Application privacy

Please provide only the information needed for recruitment. Do not include sensitive personal information that is not relevant to the role.

Before you apply

A few simple checks can help you decide whether the role is right for you and make your application easier to assess.

Read the complete role

Review the salary, location, employment type, experience level, responsibilities, and qualifications before applying.

Show relevant evidence

Share work, projects, or experience that gives us useful evidence. You do not need to match every listed skill.

Check practical details

Consider the location, working arrangement, work authorisation question, expected hours, and your preferred start date.

Request support if needed

Tell us about any adjustment you need for an interview or discussion. Asking early allows us to plan properly.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about applying, interviews, location, support, and the hiring process.

What should I include in my application?
Please include your name, contact details, current location, the role reference, a short account of relevant experience, and a link to your work when available. A clear and honest application is more useful than a long one.
How long will the process take?
We aim to acknowledge every application within seven days. The full process may take longer when practical discussions, checks, or holiday periods are involved. We will keep you informed at each important stage.
Are the roles remote?
Most roles are remote friendly, while practical roles such as IT Technician may need a hybrid arrangement. The location and working expectations are stated clearly in every listing.
Can you support work authorisation?
Please answer the work authorisation question truthfully. Where a role may require support, this will be discussed early. Any possible arrangement is subject to the requirements of the role and must be confirmed in writing.
When will I be asked for references?
We only request references when they are relevant to the role and normally after an initial discussion. You will be told whose references we would like to contact and why.
Can I request an adjustment?
Yes. Tell us what support would help you take part in the process. You can mention it in your application or at any later stage. We will consider reasonable adjustments and respond with the arrangements we can provide.
Is a portfolio required for every role?
A portfolio is useful for design and some technical roles, but it is not required for every position. Evidence of relevant work, thoughtful questions, and clear communication can be just as valuable.
What if there is no role that matches me?
You can select Open application in the form. Tell us about your strengths, the kind of work you want, and where you could contribute to XPDevs. We will keep your details in mind for a relevant opportunity.
