Career Progression

Year 1

Learn the fundamentals, complete training, and gain workplace experience.

Year 2-3

Move into a mid-level role with increased responsibility and independence.

Year 4-6

Lead projects, mentor new employees, and specialise in key areas.

Year 7+

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience?

Most junior jobs focus on potential and willingness to learn rather than extensive experience.

Can students apply?

Many employers offer internships, apprenticeships, and part-time junior opportunities.

Do I need qualifications?

Requirements vary, but many roles accept self-taught candidates with strong skills and portfolios.

Can I work remotely?

Many modern organisations offer hybrid or fully remote entry-level positions.
